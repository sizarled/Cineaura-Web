Privacy Policy

What CineAura collects, why it collects it, and the control you keep over your data.

Last updated October 11, 2026

On this page

    The short version

    We store your account details, your watch activity and what you publish. We never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time.

    • We collect only what the service needs: an email, a username, a password hash and your activity.
    • Watch minutes are counted because they feed the points system.
    • Catalog data comes from TMDB. Your searches are not sent to advertisers.
    • You can ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion from the support page.

    Who we are

    CineAura is a discovery and streaming platform for movies, series and live IPTV channels. This policy covers the website, the member dashboard, the staff panel and every page linked from them.

    What we collect

    Five kinds of data reach our servers:

    • Account data — email, username, password hash, plan, activation codes, country, chosen language.
    • Profile data — avatar, bio and the followers, follows and blocks you create.
    • Activity data — watch minutes, published watch links, playlists, recommendations, reviews, comments, shares, points and prize entries.
    • Technical data — browser and device type, approximate country from your IP address, and errors.
    • Messages — what you send through the contact form, WhatsApp or Telegram, and the reports you file.

    Why we use it

    • To run your account, your plan and your activation codes.
    • To count watch minutes and turn them into points.
    • To run prizes, verify conditions and contact winners.
    • To moderate links, comments and reports, and to apply bans when the rules are broken.
    • To improve the catalog, the player and the pages members use most.

    Points, watch time and prizes

    Watch minutes for a Free member are counted only while ads are visible; premium members can switch counting off in the site settings. Points and prize entries stay attached to your member ID so a win can be verified later.

    Delivering a prize may need a phone number or an address. That information is used only for the delivery and is deleted once the draw is closed.

    Cookies and local storage

    CineAura keeps your session, language, theme, country and ad-free preference in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data signs you out and resets those choices.

    Advertising partners may place their own cookies when an ad is displayed. Blocking ads stops that — and stops watch minutes from being counted on a Free plan.

    Third parties

    • TMDB — posters, titles and cast come from The Movie Database. Your search terms reach TMDB as a normal API request.
    • Supabase — our database and account host. Account and activity data are stored there.
    • Advertising — Adsterra serves banners and popunders to Free members only.
    • Stream sources — watch links and IPTV playlists are played from member or public servers, not from ours.
    • WhatsApp and Telegram are independent services with their own privacy policies.

    What we never do

    • We do not sell your personal data.
    • We do not read your messages to staff in order to target ads.
    • We do not show your email address to other members.
    • We do not keep a permanent record of every page you open beyond what the points system needs.

    How long we keep it

    Account and activity data stay as long as your account is open. Deleted comments, links and reports disappear from the public pages at once and from backups within 30 days.

    When you ask us to close your account we erase the profile, the points and the prize history, and keep only what the law requires.

    Security

    Passwords are stored as hashes, every request runs over HTTPS, and staff accounts only see what their role needs. No system is perfect — treat your activation and recovery codes as secrets.

    Your rights

    Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

    • show you every piece of data we hold about you;
    • correct anything inaccurate;
    • delete your account and its history;
    • export your data in a readable file;
    • object to one specific use, such as being contacted about a prize.

    Send the request from the Support & Contact page using your account email. We answer within 30 days.

    Children

    CineAura is not intended for children under 16. If we learn that an account belongs to a younger child, we close it and delete the data.

    Changes to this policy

    When this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Important changes are announced on the home page and in the member dashboard.

    Contact

    Privacy questions go to the support team through the Support & Contact page, WhatsApp or Telegram.

    Still need a hand?

    The support team answers the contact form, WhatsApp and Telegram within a few hours.
