Frequently asked questions

Most questions are answered here before you need to write to us.

How do I activate my account?

Register, then open the Activation page. Free starts with a progress bar; Silver, Gold and Diamond need a one-time activation code. Your membership time starts the moment the code is entered.

A stream keeps buffering or will not play.

Try another watch link on the same title, reopen the channel list and check your connection. If every link is dead, report the title and the link you used — we hide dead links.

Why are my watch minutes not counted?

Minutes count only for Free members while ads are visible. An ad blocker, a hidden ad slot or the ad-free setting of a premium plan stops the counting.

How do I earn points?

Watch titles, publish watch links, invite new members, build playlists, write recommendations and comment. Every rate is listed in the Terms of Service, and your dashboard shows the breakdown.

How do I enter a prize?

Open the Prizes page, choose a prize your plan allows, read its conditions and join. Points spent to join are not refunded if you withdraw later.

Can I change my language, theme or country?

Yes. Open Site Settings in the header (once you are signed in: Dashboard > Settings): language, dark or light appearance, country and the ad-free option are all there. Your choices are saved in this browser.

How do I report a broken, stolen or misleading link?

Use the Report button on the watch page, or write to us here with the title, the link and what is wrong. Reports land directly in the staff panel.

I lost my password.

Open the recovery page and paste the recovery code you saved when you registered. It sets a new password and lets you download your account details.

How do I delete my account?

Send a request from this page using your account email. We close the account, erase the profile, the points and the prize history, and answer within 30 days.
