About CineAura

What the platform does, how a member moves through it, and where the rules and the help live.

On this page

    What CineAura is

    CineAura brings movies, series and live IPTV channels into one glass-tinted interface. The catalog is powered by The Movie Database, the watch links and playlists come from members and public sources, and the video itself is streamed from those sources — never from our servers.

    Everything is built around one member account: a plan, an access code, points earned from activity and prizes you can enter.

    How it works

    Register and activate

    Create the account, save the recovery code, then activate a plan. Free needs no code; Silver, Gold and Diamond need a one-time code.

    Find something to watch

    Search the catalog by title, actor or director, browse movies and series with filters, or jump into the free and premium IPTV channel lists.

    Watch and collect points

    Every minute watched counts, and so do published links, playlists, recommendations, comments and members you invite.

    Spend points on prizes

    The prize center lists open prizes with their conditions, their modes and the time you have. Join, complete the conditions and confirm a win from the same page.

    House rules in one paragraph

    One account per person, links you are allowed to share, no bots and no farming of points, no insults, and respect for the moderators' decisions. The full text sits in the Terms of Service, and what we store is described in the Privacy Policy.

    Credits and data

    Posters, titles, cast and ratings come from The Movie Database. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

    Accounts, points, prizes and moderation run on Supabase. Video playback is served by the source behind each watch link or channel.

    Seven languages

    CineAura runs in English, Arabic, French, German, Spanish, Dutch and Italian. Switch from Site Settings in the header (Dashboard > Settings once you are signed in) — the whole interface, including these pages, follows your choice.

    Get help

    Anything not covered here goes to the support team, which answers the contact form, WhatsApp and Telegram within a few hours.
