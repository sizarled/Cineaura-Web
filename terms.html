Terms of Service

The rules of using CineAura: accounts, plans, points, prizes, published links and what our team may moderate.

Last updated October 11, 2026

On this page

    Accepting these terms

    By creating an account or by watching anything on CineAura you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

    Your account and access codes

    • One person, one account. Your username is locked after activation and cannot be transferred.
    • Activation codes work once. Membership time starts the moment a code is entered.
    • Keep your password and your recovery code private. Anything done with them counts as done by you.

    Membership plans

    Four plans exist: Free at $0 per month, Silver at $5 for 3 months, Gold at $10 for 6 months and Diamond at $20 for 12 months. Free and premium plans all need a registered account.

    Premium plans unlock the premium IPTV lineup and ad-free viewing. A plan expires at the end of its period; renew it with a new activation code.

    Points

    Points come from activity: 1 minute watched = 1 point, 5 new members through your links = 1 point, 500 published watch links = 1 point, 10 playlists = 1 point, 50 recommendations = 1 point, 100 comments = 1 point.

    Points have no cash value, cannot be transferred to another member and cannot be exchanged for money. Available points are what you earned minus what you spent on prizes.

    Prizes and contests

    • Each prize lists its own conditions, its mode and the time you have to complete them. Points paid to join are not refunded if you withdraw.
    • Winners must confirm the win inside the prize page. Fake accounts, automated activity or shared links used to farm points lead to disqualification.
    • A prize may be limited to a plan, a country or a number of winners. Those limits are shown on the prize before you join.

    Acceptable use

    You agree not to:

    • publish links to malicious, illegal or stolen content, or to content you know is fake;

    • use bots, scripts or several accounts to inflate points or win prizes;
    • probe, overload or try to break the site, the database or the staff panel;
    • impersonate a staff member or another member.

    Content you publish

    Watch links, playlists, recommendations, reviews and comments stay yours. By publishing them you give CineAura the right to display, order, translate the interface around them and remove them.

    Publish only links you are allowed to share. Our moderators can hide, edit or delete any post, and a repeated breach leads to a ban.

    Third-party streams and IPTV

    CineAura does not host video files. Watch links and IPTV playlists point to servers run by members or by public sources, and playback happens between your device and those servers.

    A channel or a link can stop working at any moment because it depends on a source we do not control. Report a broken link and we will hide it; we cannot guarantee replacement or uptime.
